Data sources, assumptions and limits (read before relying on the numbers)
  • Pipe, ASME B36.10M. Outside diameters and wall thicknesses by schedule. Painted area is π × OD per metre of pipe; the optional internal surface and the steel mass use the wall thickness. Fittings, valves and flanges are not in the database: use the area allowance.
  • Structural steel. The SAISC Red Book is a paid publication and nothing was copied from it. The section ranges are the standard UB/UC (BS 4), IPE/HE (DIN 1025), PFC, UPN (DIN 1026), angle (EN 10056) and hollow-section (EN 10210, EN 10219) series. Surface area per metre is computed from each section's dimensions and checked against published figures. Check a section against the current Red Book or your mill's catalogue if it matters; sections not listed (small tube, tees, flats, plate, welded sections) go in as Custom member with their perimeter.
  • Paint products. Volume solids and recommended film thickness come from supplier data-sheet extracts (Jotun, Carboline and PPG). The supplier websites could not be opened directly, so every product carries a note of what was seen (hover the product name). Data sheets change and many products exist in variants: confirm each against the supplier's current data sheet.
  • ISO 12944. The standard itself is paid and was not read directly. The category descriptions are the standard's own wording in short; the reference total film thickness for each category/durability has a stated basis (shown under the system table). The layer plans are generic builds that meet that reference with the listed products, not any supplier's certified system. Use the supplier's ISO 12944-6 certificate for a specification. Not covered: C1, immersion (Im1–Im4), low durability for C2/C3, galvanized or metal-sprayed steel.
  • Arithmetic. Litres = area × DFT ÷ (10 × volume solids %), then ÷ (1 − loss). One coat of 100 µm at 80 % solids covers 8 m²/L theoretically. Wet film = DFT ÷ solids. Quantities are rounded up to whole litres only in the "to order" column; pack sizes are not modelled.
  • Not modelled: pack sizes and prices, thinner, mist coats on zinc silicate, over-thickness, shop versus field application, galvanizing.
